Three groups on one rail.

Senders

Migrant workers in Singapore

Domestic, construction, service and shipyard workers who send money home regularly.

Receivers

Families back home

Households that rely on it for food, rent, school fees and medicine, including those far from banks.

Last mile

Cash-out partners

Licensed remittance outlets, e-wallet providers and community agents who earn a fair fee.

The worker only sees a normal app. The blockchain is the plumbing.

Remittance Rail03 / 11
Remittance RailThe solution

Send, move, receive, with the cost shown up front.

STOP 1 / SEND

Pay in SGD

Through a licensed on-ramp. The app shows the exact amount the family receives before you confirm.

STOP 2 / MOVE

Settle on-chain

A regulated stablecoin on a low-fee public chain. Minutes, any day of the week.

STOP 3 / RECEIVE

Collect locally

Local currency in a mobile wallet, or cash at a partner agent with a one-time code or QR.

Protect

A smart contract holds the transfer until it is collected. If not collected in time, it returns to the sender automatically.

See

A receipt shows amount sent, fee, rate used and amount received, on a public ledger.

Remittance Rail05 / 11
Remittance RailUse case

A domestic worker sends money to her mother.

Monday / Singapore

She opens the app, enters the amount, and sees exactly what her mother will receive. She confirms.

Minutes later

The transfer settles on-chain. A code arrives on her mother's phone, and the funds sit in escrow.

Same day / Philippines

Her mother collects cash at a nearby agent, or in her mobile wallet. Both sides hold the same receipt.
