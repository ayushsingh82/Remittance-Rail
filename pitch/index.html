Remittance RailThe social challenge

Part of every paycheck leaks away before it reaches home.

Illustration of where value leaks. Not measured data.

1

Hidden costs

A visible fee plus an exchange-rate margin nobody explains.

2

Slow delivery

Hours or days, and cash-out often means a trip to a pickup point.

3

No visibility

If money is delayed or lost, the sender has little to go on.

4

Hits the poorest most

A fixed fee takes a bigger share of small, frequent transfers.

Every dollar lost to fees is a dollar not spent on a child's school or a parent's medicine.

Remittance RailTarget audience

Three groups, one rail.

Senders

Domestic, construction, service and shipyard workers who send money home regularly.

Receivers

Households that rely on it for food, rent, school fees and medicine, including those far from banks.

Last mile

Licensed outlets, e-wallet providers and community agents who earn a fair fee.

Remittance Rail03 / 12
Remittance RailThe solution

Five steps from paycheck to proof.

Send

Pay in SGD through a licensed on-ramp. The app shows exactly what the family receives before you confirm.

Move

A regulated stablecoin on a low-fee public chain. Settles in minutes, any day.

Receive

Local currency in a mobile wallet, or cash at a partner agent with a one-time code or QR.

Protect

A smart contract holds the money until it is collected, or refunds the sender automatically.

See

A receipt shows amount sent, fee, rate used and amount received, on a public ledger.

The worker only sees a normal app. The blockchain is the plumbing.

Remittance Rail06 / 12
Remittance RailUse case

A domestic worker sends money to her mother.

She sends

She enters the amount and sees exactly what her mother will receive. Then she confirms.

It settles

The transfer settles on-chain. A code reaches her mother's phone, and the funds sit in escrow.

Mother collects

Cash at a nearby agent, or in a mobile wallet. Both sides hold the same receipt.

Nothing is hidden. The same four numbers appear before she confirms and again on a public receipt afterwards, so the cost can be checked by her, her mother, or anyone.

Remittance RailRisks, why now, the ask

We name the hard parts, and what we need.

Five risks, five answers
1

Licensing

Operate through a licensed partner first, seek our own licence later.

2

Stablecoin depeg

Regulated, fully reserved coins. Convert promptly. Cap exposure.

3

Ramp costs eat savings

Negotiate rates, use low-fee chains, publish the all-in cost.

4

KYC and anti-money-laundering

Full identity checks and monitoring through licensed partners.

5

First-time user trust

Familiar app, agent-assisted onboarding, employer and NGO partners.
